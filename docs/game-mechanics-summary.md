# Game Mechanics Summary

## Core Inspiration: AoE II + Settlers II + Empire Earth + Minecraft

CraftPires combines the best mechanics from these legendary games into a unified voxel-based RTS experience.

---

## From Age of Empires II

### Peasant/Villager System
- **Unlimited peasants** based on housing capacity and food resources
- **Multi-role workers**: Miners, builders, farmers, lumberjacks, soldiers
- **Commander controls all** (strategic hotkeys, task assignment)
- **Economic foundation**: Peasants are the backbone of your civilization

### Resource Economy
- **Four primary resources**: Food, Wood, Stone, Gold (plus rare materials)
- **Gather rates**: Balance between different resource types
- **Storage buildings**: Town Centers, Storage Pits, warehouses
- **Trade system**: Markets enable resource trading

### Military System
- **Barracks trains military units** from peasant population
- **Unit upgrades**: Blacksmith improvements (attack, armor)
- **Counter-units**: Rock-paper-scissors combat (infantry > archers > cavalry > infantry)
- **Siege weapons**: Trebuchets, catapults, rams

### Age Advancement
- **Tech progression**: Feudal → Castle → Imperial Ages
- **Age requirements**: Buildings + resources + research time
- **Unlocks**: New units, buildings, technologies per age

### Hotkey Control
- **Select all peasants**: Idle worker button
- **Military formations**: Line, box, staggered
- **Building placement**: Quick access to structures
- **Unit production**: Queue multiple units

## From The Settlers II

### Physical Resource Hauling
- **Peasants physically carry** materials between buildings
- **Visible transport**: See voxel clumps being moved
- **Supply chains**: Mines → Storage → Construction sites
- **Road efficiency**: Paths reduce travel time

### Construction Phases
- **Foundation stage**: Mark building location, gather materials
- **Material delivery**: Peasants bring wood, stone, etc.
- **Construction progress**: Build in visible stages (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)
- **Tool requirements**: Peasants need appropriate tools per stage

### Worker AI
- **Autonomous task-finding**: Idle peasants auto-assign to nearby needs
- **Priority system**: Critical tasks first (food shortage → farming)
- **Efficient pathing**: Workers find shortest routes
- **Load balancing**: Distribute workers across tasks

### Logistics Chains
- **Raw materials**: Wood from forests, ore from mines
- **Processing**: Smelters refine ore, sawmills process wood
- **Distribution**: Warehouses store and distribute goods
- **Supply routes**: Optimize paths for efficiency

### Visual Feedback
- **Satisfying visuals**: Watch buildings grow from foundation to completion
- **Progress indicators**: See material stockpiles at construction sites
- **Worker animations**: Peasants carry, build, mine with purpose
- **Completion moments**: Buildings "snap" into final form when done

## From Empire Earth

### Epoch Progression
**CraftPires uses 3 Ages** (simplified from Empire Earth's 14):

**Age 1: Primitive Age**
- Wood and stone tools
- Basic structures (huts, storage pits)
- Hunter-gatherer economy
- Militia infantry (spears, clubs)
- **Unlock requirements**: 500 food + 2 houses

**Age 2: Metal Age**
- Iron tools and weapons
- Advanced structures (barracks, markets, walls)
- Farming and mining economy
- Professional military (swordsmen, archers, cavalry)
- Siege weapons (catapults, rams)
- **Unlock requirements**: 1,000 food + 800 stone + 200 gold + barracks

**Age 3: Gunpowder/Crystal Age**
- Diamond/advanced material tools
- Monumental structures (wonders, fortresses)
- Complex economy (trade, specialization)
- Elite military (knights, siege engineers)
- Explosive weapons (trebuchets with gunpowder, bombards)
- Crystal-powered structures (emerald buffs, diamond armor)
- **Unlock requirements**: 2,000 food + 1,500 stone + 500 gold + 100 iron + castle

### Civilization Bonuses
- **Resource-based**: Access to rare materials determines your civ's strength
- **Tech branching**: Choose military, economic, or diplomatic specialization
- **Commander upgrades**: Material mastery (Iron, Diamond, Emerald, Obsidian)

### Long-Term Strategy
- **Year-long seasons**: Plan across epochs for end-game dominance
- **Tech investment**: Early choices impact late-game capabilities
- **Wonder victory**: Monumental projects as win condition

## From Minecraft

### Voxel World Manipulation
- **Destructible terrain**: Every voxel can be mined, moved, placed
- **Creative building**: Design unique structures with gathered materials
- **Underground exploration**: Dig tunnels, create subterranean bases
- **Terrain modification**: Reshape landscape for strategic advantage

### Material Properties
- **Material strength**: Diamond > Iron > Stone > Wood > Dirt
- **Special properties**: Obsidian (fire-resistant), Emerald (trade bonus), Coal (smelting fuel)
- **Crafting logic**: Combine materials for tools, weapons, structures

### Physics Interactions
- **Water + Dirt = Mud**: Slow enemy units, create sinkholes
- **Lava + Water = Obsidian**: Create fire-resistant structures
- **Gravity**: Unsupported blocks fall (collapse tunnels, drop traps)
- **Fire spread**: Flammable materials burn (wood, wool)

### Creative Expression
- **Unique civilizations**: Every civ looks different based on materials used
- **Aesthetic customization**: Dye wool, create patterns, build monuments
- **Experimentation**: Test physics contraptions, discover combinations

## The CraftPires Hybrid Formula

### Economic Gameplay (AoE II + Settlers II)
1. **Commander starts** with basic resources in inventory
2. **Build Town Center** (costs materials, Settlers-style construction)
3. **Spawn first peasants** (costs food)
4. **Assign peasants**: Mining, lumber, farming
5. **Gather resources**: Peasants collect and store materials
6. **Build housing**: Unlock more population capacity
7. **Construct specialized buildings**: Barracks, markets, smelters
8. **Physical hauling**: Peasants carry materials to construction sites
9. **Supply chain optimization**: Efficient routes reduce build time

### Military Gameplay (AoE II + Empire Earth)
1. **Train military units** from peasant population (barracks)
2. **Age advancement** unlocks better units
3. **Upgrade equipment**: Blacksmith improvements (iron tools → diamond weapons)
4. **Form armies**: Select units, create formations (AoE II hotkeys)
5. **Siege warfare**: Build trebuchets, load with voxel ammunition
6. **Tactical combat**: Counter-units, terrain advantages, Commander aura
7. **Capture resources**: Destroy enemy buildings, reclaim 60-70% materials

### Building Gameplay (Settlers II + Minecraft)
1. **Design blueprint** (optional: custom voxel design in sandbox mode)
2. **Select location**: Commander places foundation marker
3. **Calculate materials**: Game shows required voxels (wood, stone, etc.)
4. **Gather materials**: Peasants mine and stockpile resources
5. **Begin construction**: Peasants carry materials to site
6. **Build in phases**: Foundation → Walls → Roof → Details
7. **Completion**: Building becomes functional when 100% complete

### Creative Gameplay (Minecraft)
1. **Experiment with physics**: Water + dirt = mud traps
2. **Design contraptions**: Wind tunnels, gravity drops, water wheels
3. **Customize aesthetics**: Dye wool, create unique civ appearance
4. **Test ideas**: Sandbox mode for safe experimentation
5. **Share creations**: Blueprint library for community designs
6. **Hilarious failures**: Black wool burns, mud backfires, peasant cannons

## Key Differentiators from Source Games

### Unlike AoE II:
- **Voxel-based** (not 2D sprites on flat terrain)
- **Physics interactions** (mud, water, gravity, fire)
- **Persistent world** (year-long seasons, no match resets)
- **Terrain modification** (reshape landscape permanently)
- **Co-op multiplayer** (10 humans per civ vs 1 player per civ)

### Unlike Settlers II:
- **Combat-focused** (RTS battles, not pure economy sim)
- **Voxel freedom** (design any structure, not preset buildings)
- **Multiplayer conflict** (PvP warfare, not sandbox building)
- **Commander unit** (hero character, not god-view only)

### Unlike Empire Earth:
- **Voxel construction** (not 3D pre-modeled buildings)
- **Material-based progression** (not time-based research)
- **Physics creativity** (not just combat simulation)
- **Persistent world** (not match-based gameplay)

### Unlike Minecraft:
- **RTS strategy** (not survival sandbox)
- **Peasant workforce** (not solo player doing everything)
- **Age progression** (not flat tech tree)
- **Team coordination** (10 players vs 1 player)
- **Year-long seasons** (not infinite worlds)

## Core Gameplay Loop

### Early Game (First Hour)
1. **Commander spawns** on flat planet with starting resources (no round planet, maps are all horizontal with variance of terrains and biome types)
2. **Place Town Center** (Settlers-style construction)
3. **Train first peasants** (AoE II economy)
4. **Gather wood and food** (survival priority)
5. **Build houses** (unlock population cap)
6. **Establish economy** (miners, lumberjacks, farmers)

### Mid Game (Hours 2-10)
1. **Age advancement** to Metal Age (Empire Earth progression)
2. **Military production** (barracks, training units)
3. **Territory expansion** (claim resource-rich areas)
4. **Trade and diplomacy** (markets, alliances)
5. **Tech research** (blacksmith upgrades, specialized buildings)
6. **First conflicts** (skirmishes over resources)

### Late Game (Weeks to Months)
1. **Wonder construction** (massive voxel projects)
2. **Advanced warfare** (siege weapons, coordinated attacks)
3. **Logistics chains** (Knights & Merchants complexity)
4. **Alliance politics** (betrayals, mega-battles)
5. **Terrain warfare** (collapse tunnels, flood valleys)
6. **Season endgame** (prestige race, victory conditions)

## Co-Op Multiplayer Structure

### Solo Play
- **1 player** controls 1 Commander + unlimited AI peasants
- **Full strategic control** (like AoE II singleplayer)
- **AI automation** when offline

### Co-Op Team (2-10 Players)
- **1 Commander** (strategic control, beam mining, respawn)
- **Up to 9 human-controlled peasants** (each player = 1 peasant character)
- **Plus unlimited AI peasants** (controlled by Commander)
- **Voice chat coordination** (real-time teamwork)
- **Role specialization**: Mining expert, builder, warrior, scout, farmer

**Example 10-Player Team:**
- Player 1: Commander (strategic overview, beam mining, coordination)
- Player 2: Master Miner (focuses on gathering rare materials)
- Player 3: Head Builder (manages construction projects)
- Player 4: Military Captain (leads army in battles)
- Player 5: Scout (explores, gathers intelligence)
- Player 6: Farmer (manages food economy)
- Player 7: Engineer (operates siege weapons)
- Player 8: Diplomat (handles alliances, trade)
- Player 9: Defender (manages walls, towers, defenses)
- Player 10: Saboteur (infiltrates enemy, plants traps)
- Plus 50-200 AI peasants doing basic tasks

## Scale & Scope

### Per Shard (512×512m world)
- **Up to 50 civilizations** maximum
- **50 to 500+ total humans** (if each civ has 1-10 players)
- **2,500 to 10,000+ peasants** (AI units across all civs)
- **Seamless terrain** (no loading screens within shard)
- **24/7 gameplay** (AI automation when players offline)

### Multiple Shards (Linked Worlds)
- **Separate game instances** (reduce server load)
- **Named shards**: "Shard Alpha", "Shard Beta", "Shard Genesis"
- **Cross-shard travel**: Load into different shards to help allies
- **Shared reputation**: Prestige, achievements carry across shards
- **Different biomes**: Ice world, desert world, volcanic world

### Year-Long Seasons
- **365-day cycle** per shard
- **Persistent world**: All changes permanent until season end
- **Hall of Legends**: Best civs immortalized
- **Season reset**: New shard begins, old world becomes "artifact"

## Win Conditions

### Prestige Victory (Most Common)
- **Territory control**: Own the most land
- **Combat dominance**: Win the most battles
- **Building achievements**: Construct wonders, monuments
- **Economic power**: Control rare resource nodes
- **Alliance leadership**: Command largest coalition

### Wonder Victory (Most Prestigious)
- **Build wonder**: Massive voxel structure (100×100×50m)
- **Defend 30 days**: Keep it standing against all attacks
- **Alliance can help**: Team effort for mega-project
- **High risk/reward**: Everyone targets you, massive prestige if successful

### Elimination Victory (Most Dramatic)
- **Destroy all enemy structures**: Total conquest
- **Kill Commander**: Capture enemy Commander's voxels
- **Claim all territory**: Own entire region
- **Rare achievement**: Extreme difficulty, legendary status
