# Seasonal Structure

## Overview

CraftPires operates on year-long seasons where each shard (512×512m world) runs for 365 days before resetting. During this time, players build civilizations, wage wars, and leave permanent scars on the landscape. At season's end, the greatest achievements are immortalized in the Hall of Legends.

---

## Season Structure

### Year-Long Cycle

**Season Duration:**
- **365 real-world days** per season
- **24/7 gameplay**: World never pauses
- **AI automation**: Civilizations continue even when players offline
- **Persistent world**: All changes permanent until season reset

**Why 365 Days?**
- **Long-term investment**: Players deeply invested in their civilizations
- **Epic storylines**: Revenge cycles, alliance drama, territorial wars span months
- **Meaningful progress**: Tech progression, Wonder construction take weeks/months
- **Community bonds**: Friendships and rivalries develop over time
- **Real stakes**: Year of work on the line creates tension

### Shard-Season Relationship

**Each Shard = One Season:**
- **Shard Alpha**: Season 1 (365 days) → Reset → Season 2 (365 days)
- **Shard Beta**: Separate season, independent timeline
- **Cross-shard travel**: Players can visit other shards mid-season

**Multiple Shards Running Simultaneously:**
- **Shard Alpha**: Month 8 of Season 2
- **Shard Beta**: Month 3 of Season 1
- **Shard Gamma**: Just launched (Day 1)
- **Players choose**: Which shard to play on

**Why Multiple Shards?**
- **Performance**: 50 civs per shard max (500+ players)
- **Fresh starts**: New players join new shards
- **Different biomes**: Ice shard vs desert shard vs forest shard
- **Unique stories**: Each shard develops its own politics

## Season Phases

### Phase 1: The Land Rush (Month 1-2)

**Objectives:**
- **Claim territory**: Find best resource nodes
- **Establish economy**: Build Town Center, houses, storage
- **Scout neighbors**: Identify potential allies and threats
- **Tech research**: Begin Age 1 upgrades

**Typical Activities:**
- **Explosive expansion**: Everyone racing to claim land
- **First conflicts**: Border disputes, resource competition
- **Alliance formation**: Players band together for protection
- **Economic focus**: 80% gathering, 20% military

**Key Milestones:**
- **First Town Center** built (Day 1-3)
- **First 10 peasants** trained (Day 1-7)
- **First house** built (Day 1-3)
- **First alliance** formed (Week 1-2)
- **First Age advancement** (Week 2-4)

### Phase 2: Expansion & Consolidation (Month 3-6)

**Objectives:**
- **Expand territory**: Claim more resource nodes
- **Build military**: Train armies, construct walls
- **Tech advancement**: Age 2 (Metal Age) unlocked
- **Diplomatic maneuvering**: Form mega-alliances

**Typical Activities:**
- **Skirmishes**: Small raids for resources
- **Border fortifications**: Walls, towers, defensive structures
- **Economic growth**: 60% gathering, 40% military
- **Trade networks**: Markets enable resource trading

**Key Milestones:**
- **First barracks** built (Month 1-2)
- **First 50 peasants** (Month 2-3)
- **First Age 2 advancement** (Month 2-4)
- **First major battle** (Month 3-5)
- **First Wonder begun** (Month 4-6)

### Phase 3: Warfare & Politics (Month 7-10)

**Objectives:**
- **Territorial conquest**: Attack enemy bases
- **Wonder construction**: Begin massive projects
- **Tech supremacy**: Age 3 (Gunpowder/Crystal) unlocked
- **Alliance warfare**: Mega-battles across regions

**Typical Activities:**
- **Siege warfare**: Trebuchets, catapults, coordinated attacks
- **Underground warfare**: Tunnel attacks, cave-in traps
- **Diplomatic betrayals**: Alliance breakups, backstabbing
- **Economic warfare**: Cut off enemy resources, blockades
- **Military focus**: 40% gathering, 60% military

**Key Milestones:**
- **First Commander assassination** (Month 6-8)
- **First Age 3 advancement** (Month 7-9)
- **First Wonder completion** (Month 8-10)
- **First civilization elimination** (Month 8-10)
- **Major alliance wars** (Month 7-10)

### Phase 4: Endgame (Month 11-12)

**Objectives:**
- **Prestige race**: Final push for season victory
- **Wonder defense**: Protect wonders for 30 days
- **Total war**: All-out conflicts for dominance
- **Legacy building**: Create monuments, epic builds

**Typical Activities:**
- **Wonder sieges**: Everyone attacks Wonder-builders
- **Desperate battles**: Last-ditch efforts for victory
- **Resource exhaustion**: Rare materials depleted
- **Final alliances**: Strange bedfellows unite against leaders
- **All-in military**: 20% gathering, 80% military

**Key Milestones:**
- **Final Wonder attempts** (Month 11-12)
- **Season winner emerges** (Month 12)
- **Hall of Legends entries** (Month 12)
- **Shard reset announcement** (Week before reset)
- **Final battles** (Last week of season)

## Victory Conditions

### 1. Prestige Victory (Most Common)

**How to Win:**
- **Accumulate most prestige** across the season
- **Multiple paths**: Combat, building, economy, diplomacy
- **Scored continuously**: Leaderboard updates in real-time

**Prestige Sources:**
```
Prestige Score = 
  Territory Points × 2 +
  Combat Points × 3 +
  Building Points × 1.5 +
  Economy Points × 1 +
  Wonder Points × 5
```

**Example:**
- **Territory**: 1,000 points (large empire)
- **Combat**: 2,000 points (many victories)
- **Building**: 1,500 points (impressive structures)
- **Economy**: 500 points (rich civilization)
- **Wonder**: 5,000 points (completed Wonder)
- **Total**: 1,000×2 + 2,000×3 + 1,500×1.5 + 500×1 + 5,000×5 = **34,250 prestige**

### 2. Wonder Victory (Most Prestigious)

**How to Win:**
- **Build Wonder** (5,000-50,000 materials, weeks to construct)
- **Defend for 30 days** (everyone will attack you)
- **Alliance can help** (team effort encouraged)

**Challenges:**
- **Massive resource sink**: Delays military development
- **Huge target**: Entire server attacks you
- **30-day defense**: Must survive constant sieges
- **High prestige reward**: 5× multiplier if successful

**Strategic Considerations:**
- **Early Wonder**: Risky (enemies stronger), more time to defend
- **Late Wonder**: Safer (stronger defenses), less time before season end
- **Secret Wonder**: Build underground, reveal at last moment
- **Decoy Wonder**: Build fake Wonder, real one hidden

### 3. Elimination Victory (Most Dramatic)

**How to Win:**
- **Destroy all enemy structures** in your region
- **Kill all enemy Commanders** (force permanent exile)
- **Claim all territory** (no one else left)

**Extremely Rare:**
- **Defender's advantage** makes total elimination difficult
- **Alliances protect**: Difficult to eliminate entire alliance
- **Cross-shard support**: Enemies can get reinforcements
- **Resource intensive**: Requires massive military commitment

**Legendary Status:**
- **Epic prestige reward**: 10,000+ prestige
- **Hall of Legends entry**: Guaranteed immortalization
- **Community recognition**: Famous forever

## Season Reset

### End of Season Process

**Final Week:**
1. **Leaderboard freeze** (7 days before reset)
2. **Final scores calculated** (all prestige sources tallied)
3. **Winners announced** (top 10 civilizations)
4. **Screenshot archive** (world documented)
5. **Hall of Legends entries** (greatest builds preserved)

**Reset Day:**
1. **Shard goes offline** (12-hour maintenance)
2. **World wiped** (all terrain, structures, resources reset)
3. **New procedural generation** (different biome layout)
4. **Players reset** (everyone starts fresh)
5. **Shard comes back online** (new season begins)

### What Resets

**World State:**
- ❌ All terrain modifications (mines, moats, craters)
- ❌ All structures (buildings, walls, wonders)
- ❌ All resources (voxels reset to procedural generation)
- ❌ All territory claims (no one owns land)

**Player Progress:**
- ❌ Commander stats (back to base HP, damage, etc.)
- ❌ Peasant count (start with 3 peasants)
- ❌ Tech tree (all research lost)
- ❌ Alliances (all diplomatic relationships reset)

**Economy:**
- ❌ Resources (no materials carried over)
- ❌ Structures (no buildings persist)
- ❌ Population (back to 3 starting peasants)

### What Persists

**Cosmetics:**
- ✅ Commander skins (carried over)
- ✅ Beam colors (carried over)
- ✅ Building textures (carried over)
- ✅ Emotes (carried over)

**Achievements:**
- ✅ Hall of Legends entries (immortalized)
- ✅ Season titles ("Season 1 Champion")
- ✅ Rare achievements (recorded)

**Starting Bonuses:**
- ✅ +10% starting resources per previous season
- ✅ Blueprint library (custom builds saved)
- ✅ Reputation (community recognition)

## Mid-Season Events

### Special Occurrences

**Resource Bonuses (Random):**
- **Diamond Rush**: 2× diamond spawn rate for 24 hours
- **Golden Week**: 2× gold spawn rate for 7 days
- **Emerald Event**: Rare emerald nodes appear

**Weather Events (Seasonal):**
- **Winter**: Snow accumulation, movement penalties
- **Spring**: Increased farming yields
- **Summer**: Drought (water scarcity)
- **Fall**: Harvest bonuses

**Community Events (Planned):**
- **Tournament Mode**: Competitive battles for prizes
- **Building Contest**: Best custom build wins prestige
- **Siege Challenge**: Defend against AI super-army
- **Roleplay Events**: Themed gameplay sessions

### Dynamic World Events

**Meteor Shower:**
- **Rare materials fall from sky** (random locations)
- **Everyone rushes to claim** (PvP hotspot)
- **Creates craters** (permanent terrain change)

**Volcanic Eruption:**
- **Lava flows destroy terrain** (permanent landscape change)
- **Creates obsidian fields** (new resource opportunities)
- **Forces evacuations** (civilizations must relocate)

**Earthquake:**
- **Collapses underground structures** (tunnels, bases)
- **Opens new cave systems** (exploration opportunities)
- **Triggers landslides** (terrain modification)

**Flood:**
- **Heavy rains flood lowlands** (temporary water)
- **Creates mud fields** (movement penalties)
- **Damages structures** (wood buildings take damage)

## Season Strategies

### Early Season (Month 1-3)

**Aggressive Strategy:**
- **Pros**: Claim best territory early, intimidate neighbors
- **Cons**: Risk early elimination, resource shortage
- **Tactics**: Rush military, raid neighbors, expand quickly

**Defensive Strategy:**
- **Pros**: Safe economy growth, strong late-game
- **Cons**: Lose best territory, vulnerable to rush
- **Tactics**: Build walls early, focus economy, tech up fast

**Diplomatic Strategy:**
- **Pros**: Alliance protection, resource sharing
- **Cons**: Dependent on allies, vulnerable to betrayal
- **Tactics**: Form alliances quickly, trade actively, support allies

### Mid Season (Month 4-8)

**Economic Focus:**
- **Pros**: Strong resource base, tech advantage
- **Cons**: Vulnerable to attacks, slower expansion
- **Tactics**: Max peasants, advanced tech, Wonder prep

**Military Focus:**
- **Pros**: Territory conquest, prestige from battles
- **Cons**: Resource drain, slower tech progression
- **Tactics**: Constant raids, siege warfare, Commander kills

**Balanced Approach:**
- **Pros**: Flexible, adaptable to any situation
- **Cons**: Not specialized, medium at everything
- **Tactics**: 50/50 economy/military, tech+military progression

### Late Season (Month 9-12)

**Wonder Rush:**
- **Pros**: Massive prestige if successful
- **Cons**: Huge target, everyone attacks you
- **Tactics**: Build Wonder ASAP, defend 30 days, all-in

**Prestige Grind:**
- **Pros**: Consistent points, less risky
- **Cons**: Requires constant activity, can be boring
- **Tactics**: Max all prestige sources, diversify scoring

**Kingmaker:**
- **Pros**: Influence season outcome, create chaos
- **Cons**: No personal victory, enemies everywhere
- **Tactics**: Attack leaders, support underdogs, create drama

## Cross-Season Progression

### Season Carryover

**What Matters Across Seasons:**
- **Reputation**: Community recognizes great players
- **Skill**: Game knowledge improves each season
- **Friendships**: Teams reform each season
- **Blueprint library**: Custom builds saved
- **Cosmetic rewards**: Skins, colors, emotes

**Starting Bonuses:**
- **Season 1 winner**: +10% starting resources Season 2
- **Season 2 winner**: +20% starting resources Season 3
- **Cumulative**: Each season win adds +10%

**Title System:**
- **"Season 1 Champion"**: Permanent title
- **"Three-Time Winner"**: Won 3 seasons
- **"Hall of Legends"**: Top 10 multiple seasons
- **"Immortal Commander"**: Never died entire season

## The Season Experience

### For New Players (First Season)

**Month 1:**
- **Learning curve**: Understand game mechanics
- **Small goals**: Build first base, survive first month
- **Find allies**: Join friendly alliance
- **Have fun**: Don't worry about winning

**Month 6:**
- **Competent**: Understand all systems
- **Established**: Strong economy, decent military
- **Active participant**: Join battles, contribute to alliances
- **Enjoy depth**: Appreciate strategic complexity

**Month 12:**
- **Experienced**: Ready for next season
- **Connections**: Made friends, formed rivalries
- **Excited**: Can't wait to start fresh with new knowledge

### For Veterans (Multiple Seasons)

**Early Season:**
- **Efficient start**: Optimal build orders, fast expansion
- **Leadership**: Form alliances, coordinate strategies
- **Reputation**: Known players get respect (and targets)

**Mid Season:**
- **Strategic depth**: Advanced tactics, creative warfare
- **Community influence**: Shape shard politics
- **Mentorship**: Help new players, build strong alliances

**Late Season:**
- **Victory push**: Execute season-long strategy
- **Epic moments**: Create legendary stories
- **Prep for next**: Already planning next season
