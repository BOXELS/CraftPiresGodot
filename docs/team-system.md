# Team System

## Overview

CraftPires is built around **team-based civilizations** where 1 Commander leads 1-9 Peasants in building, mining, and warfare. This creates both intimate solo play and epic coordinated team battles.

## Team Structure

### Commander (Civ Owner)

**Role**: Strategic leader and primary decision maker

**Capabilities**:
- **Spawns first** - Drops initial Town Center, begins civilization
- **Beam mining** - 2× gather speed, signature laser ability
- **Command aura** - +10% work rate for all nearby team members
- **Strategic control** - Build orders, resource allocation, diplomacy
- **Respawn system** - Can return at great cost (500 food + 300 stone + 200 gold + 1 diamond)

**Responsibilities**:
- Set overall strategy and goals
- Coordinate team activities
- Manage resources and construction priorities
- Handle diplomacy and alliance negotiations
- Make tactical decisions during battles

**Death Consequences**:
- **Short term**: Loss of aura, no global build permissions, reduced morale
- **Mid term**: Can still play but limited tech access
- **Respawn**: Requires massive resources, 24-hour cooldown
- **No respawn**: Empire slowly collapses without leadership

### Peasants (Team Members)

**Role**: The foundation of everything - every unit, building, and resource requires peasants

**Progression System**: Peasants evolve from weak, vulnerable workers to advanced specialists

**Stage 1: Primitive Peasants (Early Game)**
- **Walking only** - No vehicles, slow movement
- **Weak and vulnerable** - Can die from strong winds, falling into pits
- **Basic tools** - Wooden pickaxes, stone hammers
- **Limited capabilities** - Can't mine diamonds, build advanced structures
- **Hilarious failures** - "Peasant tries to mine diamond with wooden pickaxe for 3 hours, gives up"

**Stage 2: Road-Building Peasants (Feudal Age)**
- **Still walking** - But now on roads for +50% speed
- **Better tools** - Iron pickaxes, can mine basic metals
- **Road construction** - Can build stone paths
- **Moderate strength** - Survive minor environmental hazards

**Stage 3: Wheel-Discovering Peasants (Castle Age)**
- **Wheel technology** - Can build and use carts
- **Advanced tools** - Steel pickaxes, can mine gold and obsidian
- **Much stronger** - Survive most environmental hazards
- **Cart transportation** - 2× speed, 150 resource capacity

**Stage 4: Advanced Peasants (Imperial Age)**
- **Hovercraft technology** - Can build and use flying vehicles
- **Master tools** - Diamond pickaxes, can mine ANYTHING
- **Elite strength** - Survive almost all hazards
- **Hovercraft transportation** - 3× speed, 300 resource capacity

**Specialization Options**:

**Miner Peasants** (Resource Focus)
- **Primitive**: Wooden pickaxe, can only gather wood/stone
- **Road-Building**: Iron pickaxe, can gather iron/coal
- **Wheel-Discovering**: Steel pickaxe, can gather gold/obsidian
- **Advanced**: Diamond pickaxe, can mine ANYTHING

**Builder Peasants** (Construction Focus)
- **Primitive**: Wooden tools, basic structures only
- **Road-Building**: Iron tools, stone construction
- **Wheel-Discovering**: Steel tools, complex structures
- **Advanced**: Diamond tools, wonder construction

**Warrior Peasants** (Combat Focus)
- **Primitive**: Wooden weapons, weak combat
- **Road-Building**: Iron weapons, basic combat
- **Wheel-Discovering**: Steel weapons, chariot warfare
- **Advanced**: Diamond weapons, hovercraft warfare

**Scout Peasants** (Exploration Focus)
- **Primitive**: Walking, vulnerable to hazards
- **Road-Building**: Road bonus, better survival
- **Wheel-Discovering**: Cart exploration, faster mapping
- **Advanced**: Hovercraft scouting, aerial reconnaissance

**Farmer Peasants** (Economy Focus)
- **Primitive**: Basic farming, low yield
- **Road-Building**: Road access, better irrigation
- **Wheel-Discovering**: Cart transport, larger farms
- **Advanced**: Hovercraft farming, massive operations

## Team Coordination

### Communication Systems

**Voice Chat** (Primary)
- Team-only channel for real-time coordination
- Commander can broadcast to all team members
- Peasants can request resources or assistance
- **Range**: Unlimited within same region

**Text Chat** (Secondary)
- Quick messages and coordination
- Resource requests and status updates
- **Commands**: `/gather wood`, `/build barracks`, `/attack north`

**Visual Indicators** (Tertiary)
- Commander's beam shows current target
- Resource markers show gathering priorities
- Construction ghosts show planned buildings
- **Minimap**: Team member locations and status

### Resource Management

**Shared Inventory**:
- All resources go to civilization's central storage
- Commander controls allocation and distribution
- Peasants can request specific materials
- **Priority system**: Commander sets gathering priorities

**Specialization Benefits**:
- Miners gather 25% faster but can't build
- Builders construct 50% faster but gather slower
- **Balanced teams** work most efficiently
- **Solo play**: AI peasants follow Commander's specialization

### Decision Making

**Commander Authority**:
- **Strategic decisions**: What to build, where to expand
- **Resource allocation**: How to spend materials
- **Diplomacy**: Alliance negotiations, war declarations
- **Tactics**: Battle formations, siege strategies

**Peasant Input**:
- **Tactical suggestions**: "We need more wood for walls"
- **Resource requests**: "Can I get iron for better tools?"
- **Scouting reports**: "Found diamond vein to the north"
- **Combat feedback**: "Archers are vulnerable on the left"

**Consensus Building**:
- Commander can poll team on major decisions
- **Voting system** for non-critical choices
- **Override authority** for Commander in emergencies
- **Team harmony** affects overall efficiency

## Solo vs Team Play

### Solo Civilization (1 Player)

**Setup**:
- Player controls Commander + 9 AI peasants
- AI follows basic orders and Commander's specialization
- **Advantage**: Full control, no coordination issues
- **Disadvantage**: Slower than human team coordination

**AI Peasant Behavior**:
- **Gather resources** based on Commander's priorities
- **Build structures** following Commander's blueprints
- **Defend territory** using basic combat AI
- **Respond to commands** via text/voice

**AI Specialization**:
- Commander chooses AI peasant specializations
- **Balanced**: 2 miners, 2 builders, 2 warriors, 2 scouts, 1 farmer
- **Focused**: All miners for resource rush, all builders for construction
- **Custom**: Mix based on strategy

### Team Civilization (2-10 Players)

**Setup**:
- 1 Commander + 1-9 human-controlled peasants
- **Real-time coordination** through voice chat
- **Specialization** allows complex strategies
- **Advantage**: Speed, efficiency, complex tactics
- **Challenge**: Coordination, communication

**Team Dynamics**:
- **Peasant roles** are chosen by Commander or self-selected
- **Voice coordination** essential for complex operations
- **Trust and communication** crucial for success
- **Individual skill** matters within team context

**Coordination Benefits**:
- **Simultaneous actions**: Multiple peasants work together
- **Complex strategies**: Siege operations, multi-pronged attacks
- **Resource efficiency**: Specialized gathering and building
- **Tactical flexibility**: Real-time adaptation to situations

## Cross-Region Team Play

### Multi-Region Alliances

**Alliance Structure**:
- Teams can form alliances across multiple regions
- **Shared vision** across all controlled regions
- **Cross-region communication** (alliance chat)
- **Resource sharing** (caravans between regions)
- **Coordinated attacks** (multiple regions vs one)

**Team Deployment**:
- **Commander stays** in home region (strategic control)
- **Peasants can travel** to other regions
- **Temporary assignments** for specific missions
- **Permanent relocation** for long-term campaigns

### Cross-Region Warfare

**Invasion Scenarios**:
- **Team A** (Region 1) invades **Team B** (Region 2)
- **Alliance support** from multiple regions
- **Coordinated attacks** across region boundaries
- **Supply lines** between regions

**Revenge Stories**:
- Solo player bullied in Region A
- **Allies from Region B** hear the call
- **Cross-region invasion** to support underdog
- **Epic battles** with teams from multiple regions

## Balance Considerations

### Solo vs Team Balance

**Solo Advantages**:
- **Full control** - No coordination overhead
- **Consistent strategy** - No conflicting opinions
- **Faster decisions** - No discussion time
- **AI reliability** - Peasants always follow orders

**Team Advantages**:
- **Human creativity** - Peasants can suggest strategies
- **Specialization** - Each peasant excels in their role
- **Complex tactics** - Multi-pronged attacks, sieges
- **Social gaming** - Friends working together

**Balance Mechanisms**:
- **AI efficiency** - Solo AI peasants work at 80% human efficiency
- **Coordination costs** - Teams need time to coordinate
- **Resource sharing** - Teams must manage shared inventory
- **Communication overhead** - Teams need voice chat for optimal play

### Team Size Balance

**Small Teams (2-4 players)**:
- **Intimate coordination** - Easy to communicate
- **Focused strategy** - Clear roles and responsibilities
- **Fast decisions** - Quick consensus building
- **Limited specialization** - Fewer role options

**Large Teams (6-10 players)**:
- **Complex coordination** - Requires strong leadership
- **Full specialization** - All roles covered
- **Sophisticated tactics** - Multi-layered strategies
- **Communication challenges** - Voice chat management

**Optimal Team Size**: 5-7 players (1 Commander + 4-6 Peasants)

## Technical Implementation

### Team Management

```typescript
interface Civilization {
  id: string;
  commander: Player;
  peasants: Player[]; // 1-9 players
  region: string;
  resources: ResourceMap;
  structures: Structure[];
  alliance?: Alliance;
  specialization: TeamSpecialization;
}

interface Player {
  id: string;
  role: 'commander' | 'peasant';
  civId: string;
  specialization?: 'miner' | 'builder' | 'warrior' | 'scout' | 'farmer';
  online: boolean;
  voiceChannel: string;
}

interface TeamSpecialization {
  miners: number;
  builders: number;
  warriors: number;
  scouts: number;
  farmers: number;
}
```

### Communication Systems

**Voice Chat Integration**:
- **Team channels** for each civilization
- **Alliance channels** for cross-region coordination
- **Proximity chat** for nearby players
- **Commander broadcast** to all team members

**Text Command System**:
```typescript
// Peasant commands
/gather <resource> <location>
/build <blueprint> <location>
/attack <target> <formation>
/request <resource> <amount>

// Commander commands
/setpriority <resource> <high|medium|low>
/assign <peasant> <task>
/broadcast <message>
/declare <war|peace> <target>
```

### AI Peasant System

**Behavior Trees**:
- **Gather resources** based on Commander's priorities
- **Build structures** following Commander's blueprints
- **Defend territory** using basic combat AI
- **Respond to commands** via text/voice

**Specialization AI**:
- **Miner AI**: Focus on resource gathering, avoid combat
- **Builder AI**: Focus on construction, repair structures
- **Warrior AI**: Focus on combat, defend territory
- **Scout AI**: Focus on exploration, avoid danger
- **Farmer AI**: Focus on food production, manage livestock
